Exercise session · wearer: –

Calibration learns your resting and max HR as one guided session. 3 min sitting still, then 3 min easy, 2 min hard, 1 min as hard as you safely can, and 2 min standing still for recovery. The band buzzes at each change; just follow the screen. Your peak is a lower bound on true max HR. Skip the all-out minute if you have a heart condition or feel unwell.

Intensity = ENMO, the standard wrist-accelerometer measure (mean of |acceleration| − 1 g, in milli-g): roughly <50 still, 50–150 light, 150–400 moderate, >400 vigorous. Rhythm = repeats per minute of the wrist movement, about steps/min when walking, marching or shuffling. Blank when the movement isn't regular.
